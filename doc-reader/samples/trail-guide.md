# Mist Falls Trail

A **moderate** out-and-back hike to one of the tallest waterfalls in the park. Read the [park alerts](https://example.com/alerts) before you go.

![Photo of the falls](falls.jpg)

## What to bring

- Two liters of water per person
- A light jacket, since the spray near the falls is *cold*
- [x] Trail map
- Snacks for the turnaround point

## Trail at a glance

| Section | Distance | Climb |
|---|---|---|
| Trailhead to bridge | 1.2 mi | 150 ft |
| Bridge to the falls | 2.4 mi | 1,100 ft |

## Getting there

From the visitor center, take the shuttle to stop 4. Parking at the trailhead fills by 9 a.m. on weekends.

```bash
# directions for your GPS
navigate --to "Mist Falls Trailhead"
```

> Tip: the best light on the falls is in the late afternoon.
